# Types of Networks

## What is a Network Type?
A network type is classified based on the **geographical area it covers** and the **number of devices connected**.

---

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)
### What is it?
A network that connects devices within a **small area** such as a home, office, or campus.

### Characteristics
- High speed
- Low latency
- Privately owned

### Example
- Office Wi-Fi
- College computer lab

---

## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
### What is it?
A network that spans a **city or large campus**.

### Characteristics
- Medium range
- Used by ISPs and organizations

### Example
- City-wide broadband network

---

## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)
### What is it?
A network that covers a **large geographical area** like countries or continents.

### Characteristics
- Lower speed compared to LAN
- Uses public infrastructure

### Example
- The Internet

---

## 4. PAN (Personal Area Network)
### What is it?
A small network for **personal devices**.

### Example
- Bluetooth connection between phone and earbuds

---

## Comparison Table

| Network | Coverage | Example |
|------|--------|--------|
| PAN | Few meters | Bluetooth |
| LAN | Building | Office network |
| MAN | City | ISP network |
| WAN | Global | Internet |

---

## Real-World Usage
- LAN → Office, home
- WAN → Cloud services (AWS, Azure)
- PAN → Wearables

---

## Interview Points
- LAN is fast and private
- WAN uses routers and public networks
- Internet is the biggest WAN
